Our Story

Built by drivers, for drivers. Mostly for us, if we're being honest.

ClearPath Insurance was founded by three lifelong drivers who, like millions of Americans, spent years handing over hundreds of dollars a month to insurance companies that never seemed to have their best interests at heart.

So in 2014, co-founders Marcus, Dana, and Tomás sat down over coffee and asked themselves a simple question: what if, instead of paying an insurance company, we just became the insurance company?

It wasn't about disrupting an industry. It wasn't about changing lives, though we do say that in the brochure. It was about three guys who were tired of their premiums going up every six months for reasons nobody could ever really explain — and who realized that if you own the company, your premiums are basically whatever you decide they are.

Today, ClearPath proudly insures drivers across multiple states, employs dozens of people, and — most importantly — the three of us haven't personally paid an auto insurance premium since 2015.

We like to think of it as our gift to the world: proof that if enough people are frustrated enough by something, the answer isn't always to fix it. Sometimes it's to go around it.
