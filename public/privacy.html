Privacy Policy

Last updated: whenever we last remembered to.

ClearPath Insurance Agency, LLC ("ClearPath," "we," "us") takes your privacy seriously, in that we are seriously aware it is a topic that exists.

We'll be straightforward with you, which is more than most privacy policies offer: nobody here has actually read the relevant statutes. Not the federal ones, not the state ones, and certainly not whatever Delaware requires of a company domiciled there. We looked into it briefly in 2016, found it long, and moved on.

Frankly, we don't think it's that important. You're applying for car insurance, not defusing a bomb. We collect what the form asks for, we keep it somewhere, and we assume that's roughly what everyone else in this industry does too, because we have never once checked.

What we collect

Whatever you type into the form. Possibly some things about your browser we didn't go looking for but that showed up anyway. We are not totally sure, and have not been totally sure since 2014.

Who we share it with

We assume "affiliates and partners," because that's what it says in every other policy we've skimmed. We have not sat down and made a list.

Your rights

You're welcome to ask us to delete your data. Someone will see the email eventually.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if someone brings it up again, which, going by the last update date above, isn't often.

ClearPath Insurance: your privacy, we assume, is fine.
