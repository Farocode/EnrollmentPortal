About ClearPath

A note from Corporate Communications.

ClearPath Insurance Agency, LLC was founded in 2014 and has, through sheer persistence, remained a going concern ever since.

The company is currently the subject of eleven active lawsuits across four states, a formal inquiry from the Delaware Department of Insurance, and an open file with the Federal Trade Commission, the details of which we are not permitted to discuss.

None of this has slowed us down. ClearPath proudly employs dozens(!) of people out of our headquarters in Wilmington, Delaware, supported by offshore claims-processing operations in Madagascar and a satellite customer support office in Tehran, Iran, whose operating status changes from quarter to quarter for reasons outside our control.
